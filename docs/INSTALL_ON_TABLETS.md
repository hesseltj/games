# Putting Brainy Town on the kids' tablets

The app isn't a file on your phone. It's a website that installs itself like an app. So "moving it"
to a tablet means **opening the same web address on the tablet and installing it there**. Your kids'
progress (coins, houses, levels) can come along too, through the family cloud backup.

## Step 1: Find your app's web address (1 minute)

It's the address Netlify gave your site, something like `https://brainy-town-family.netlify.app`.
- **Netlify:** log in at <https://app.netlify.com>, click your site, and copy the link shown under its name.
- **Your iPhone:** if you added the app from Safari, the address is in Safari's history.

Send it to yourself in a text or email so you can tap it on each tablet instead of typing it.

## Step 2: Decide about the progress on your phone

| If… | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Cloud backup is set up** (you followed `CLOUD_SETUP.md`) | On your phone, open the app → **⚙️ Grown-ups** → **Settings** → make sure it says **Up to date ✓** under Cloud backup. Everything you made on the phone is now saved online. |
| **Cloud backup isn't set up**, and you'd like to bring the players over | Set it up first (`CLOUD_SETUP.md`, about 15 minutes), then sign in on your phone as above. |
| **You don't need the test progress** | Skip ahead. Each kid will make a fresh player on their own tablet. |

## Step 3: Install on each Android tablet (2 minutes per tablet)

1. Open **Chrome** on the tablet and go to your app's address (tap the link you texted or emailed yourself).
2. Tap **⋮** (top right) → **Install app** (on some tablets: **Add to Home screen** → **Install**).
3. Close Chrome. Open **Brainy Town** from the home screen. It opens full-screen like any app.

**If you set up cloud backup:**

4. On the first screen ("What's your name?"), tap **⚙️ Grown-ups: restore players from cloud backup** at the bottom.
5. Answer the grown-ups question, then under **Cloud backup** enter your family email and password and tap **Sign in**.
6. When it says **Up to date ✓**, tap **←**. All the players appear.
7. Hand the tablet to its owner and have them **tap their own picture**. From then on the tablet opens straight to them.

**If you didn't set up cloud backup:** just type the kid's name on the first screen and make their character.

## Step 4: Set up each tablet (optional, 2 minutes)

In **⚙️ Grown-ups → Settings** on each tablet:
- **Voice:** pick the voice your kid likes best and tap **▶ Test voice**. Voices marked "Natural" or "Enhanced" usually sound best.
- **Daily play time:** set a limit if you want one, for example 30 minutes.
- **Music during games:** turn it off if a kid finds it distracting. The voice always stays on.

### Get the best voice on Android

The spoken instructions use the tablet's built-in voice. For the nicest one:
1. Open the tablet's **Settings** → search for **Text-to-speech**.
2. Set **Preferred engine** to **Speech Services by Google**. If it's missing, install it from the Play Store.
3. Tap the ⚙️ next to it → **Install voice data** → **English (United States)** → download a voice and pick your favorite.
4. Back in Brainy Town, choose that voice in Grown-ups → Settings → Voice.

## Good to know

- **Updates are automatic.** When a new version is published, close the app completely and reopen it. If it still looks old, close and reopen once more.
- **Offline play works.** Once installed and opened online once, games work without Wi-Fi. Progress saves on the tablet and uploads to the cloud next time it's online.
- **Google Family Link:** if the tablets are managed by Family Link, you may need to allow Chrome, or approve the site, in the Family Link app first.
- **Amazon Fire tablets** don't come with Chrome. Use the built-in **Silk** browser: open the address, then open the menu → **Add to Home**.
- **Don't clear Chrome's data or uninstall the app** on a tablet without cloud backup, or that kid's progress is lost. With cloud backup, just sign in again.
- **Your phone's test players:** with cloud backup they're shared with the family account, so you'll see them on the tablets too. Simply ignore any test player you don't want.
